# 📜 Agreement Analysis: Penalties & Fines

## 🔍 Found Clauses

| Section | Original Text | Plain English Meaning |
|---------|---------------|-----------------------|
| 5.1 | "В случае нарушения пункта 2.3 Пользователь обязуется выплатить штраф в размере 1000 (одной тысячи) долларов США в течение 10 (десяти) рабочих дней." | If you violate section 2.3, you must pay a $1,000 fine within 10 business days. |
| 5.2 | "За каждый день просрочки платежа начисляется пеня в размере 0.5% от суммы штрафа. Общий размер пени не может превышать 30% от суммы основного долга." | For every day you're late on payment, a 0.5% penalty is added to the fine. However, the total penalties can never exceed 30% of your main debt amount. |

## 🧾 What This Means For You

1. **Hard Deadline**: You have exactly 10 working days to pay the $1,000 penalty after breaking rule 2.3. No extensions mentioned.
2. **Daily Interest**: While you're paying, an extra 0.5% per day keeps stacking up like a snowball.
3. **Cap Exists**: The law protects you here — even with all the late fees added together, they legally cannot take more than 30% total from what you originally owed.

## 💡 Bottom Line
- Break clause 2.3 → owe $1000 flat
- Pay late → 0.5% daily fee grows your debt
- But breathe easy → that growth stops at 30% max
